## How to read this page

{The G4 fixed block, copied exactly from README.md.}

**The columns used on this page:**
- **ID** - the decision's lasting id. Other pages link to it, for example `decision-register.md#d-7`.
- **Term** - the term or terms the decision settled, each a link to its definition. It is `-` where it settled a rule rather than a term.
- **Where** - the section the question came from, as a link to its evidence in `deep-dives/`, then the source items. It is **All sections** when no single section owns it.
- **Decided** - the question that was settled.
- **Chosen** - the word, split, or answer taken. Each term is a link to its definition.
- **Retired / overrides** - the retired words, or the earlier decision reversed.
- **Basis** - the reason, quoting or citing the sources.
- **Gate row** - the row in the gate log where the question was raised, with its evidence and options.
- **Date** - the day it was settled.
