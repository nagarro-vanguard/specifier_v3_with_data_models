# States and values

<One short paragraph: the named conditions a term can be in, and the closed sets of values - all <n> of them - grouped by the same families as the rest of the glossary, each with the term it belongs to.>

## How to read this page

<The G4 fixed block, copied exactly from README.md.>

**The columns used on this page:**
- **Value-set** - the agreed name for the condition, or for the set of values.
- **What it is** - the definition.
- **Belongs to** - the term this condition is a property of, as a link to its definition; `-` when it belongs to no single term.
- **Values** - the allowed members of the set, one per line; `-` when the term is one condition, not a set.
- **Notes** - a fixed note where the sources write the set's members as if they were kinds of it; otherwise `-`.
- **Source** and **Decision** - where the sources define it, and the decision that settled it.

**The columns of Terms replaced or retired over time:**
- **Earlier word** - a word the glossary no longer uses.
- **What happened** - one of:
  - renamed
  - merged
  - split
  - withdrawn
- **Use now** - the current term to use instead, as a link to its definition; `-` when the word was withdrawn with nothing in its place.
- **Decision** - a link to the decision that replaced or retired it.
- **Date** - the date of that decision.
