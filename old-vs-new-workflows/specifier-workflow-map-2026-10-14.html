Specifier Workflow Map

From the client's documents to ready stories. Every artifact stays, because together they are the audit trail. The cuts are in the workflow: fewer steps and fewer stops.

Three kinds of file

Living documentKept current. Each fact has one home among them. Anywhere else, a fact appears as a reference by ID or as a word-for-word copy a program checks.
RecordWritten at the time and never rewritten. This is the audit trail: how each decision was reached.
Generated fileMade by a program from living documents. Never edited by hand.

Ground rules

  • Every living document carries its own decision record.
  • Look before you ask: the sources first, then the decision records of everything the step reads.
  • A later step never edits an accepted document. It sends the problem back.
  • Nothing is invented. Every line cites a source or a decision.
  • Words are agreed before features are written.
  • Phases 1 to 3 cover the whole product once. Phase 4 runs one EPIC at a time.
  • Stories are assembled from decisions already made.

What changed in this version

Cuts in the workflow

  1. probe-requirements now does the intake. It lists the sources and which one wins. Same reading, and "which source wins" is just another question.
  2. write-brd no longer runs its own discovery round. It starts from the Discovery Brief and asks only what is still open. Before, it asked again what the probe had asked.
  3. generate-epics has no approval stop for the EPIC Register. The Register is a checked copy of the clustering option you already picked. Approving a copy adds nothing.
  4. frd-author has no scope stop. Done-when clauses and capabilities are settled once, in the Feature catalog, instead of once per FRD.
  5. Design links are attached in draft-stories. A lookup does not need a step of its own.
  6. groom-stories and write-acceptance-criteria have no stops. They assemble each story from decided pieces. The coverage gate and one ready mark per EPIC replace a gate per batch of four stories and a gate per story.
  7. Change management has no separate sorting step. change-impact already classifies each change.
  8. Sources are read only in Phase 1. After that, each step reads its nearest input and looks up the rest by ID. If something is missing, it goes back to the PRD.

Artifacts

None are cut. They are the audit trail. What changed is how they relate:

  • Each file is now one of the three kinds above.
  • Sections that repeated another document now point to it:
    • BRD: NFRs and the user journey move to the PRD
    • PRD: scope moves to the BRD
    • PRD: per-feature business rules move to the FRDs
    • PRD: data requirements move to the Glossary (names) and the FRDs (what each capability keeps)
    • Glossary: role terms point to the PRD personas
    • Glossary: step terms carry names, not order
    • Story grooming sections point to the FRD, Feature catalog and BRD
  • EPIC sequencing and EPIC mapping are generated, never hand-written.
  • EPIC detail is optional. It holds only EPIC-level success measures and NFRs.
  • The PRD holds every requirement word for word, numbered, with no priority and no grouping. Priority and capabilities live in the Feature catalog, which points to the requirements by number.

Used inside every step

Recording a decision

Runs whenever a step needs something only the specifiers can settle.

1
A step needs something decided.
2
Look first in the sources and in the decision records of everything the step reads.
Found: cite it. No question is asked.
3
Write the question in this document's decision record, with why it matters, the options, and whether it blocks.
4
The specifiers answer. The answer is recorded word for word, with the date.
5
Act on the answer, as in the table below.
6
Close the entry by listing every place its answer is used.
The answerWhat happens
A clear answerApply it. Everything built on it cites its number.
"Assume X"Apply it as an assumption. It is flagged wherever it is used.
"Out of scope"Record it, leave it out here, and send it back to the BRD's scope.
It conflicts with a sourceDecide which one wins, and record the winner.
It raises a new questionWrite a new entry, linked to this one, and go back to step 3.
"Not yet"Leave it open, with a date.
  • Doesn't block: carry on, marked TBD.
  • Blocks: the specifiers choose one of these:
    • wait
    • assume
    • go ahead at risk

Sending back

Runs when a step finds a gap in a document written earlier.

1
A step finds a gap in an earlier document.
2
Raise it in that document's decision record. The earlier document is never edited from here.
3
Route it by the state of that document.
Not yet accepted: its own step settles it.
Already accepted: it goes through change management.
4
The step that found it waits, or carries on if the gap doesn't block it.

The decision record

Every living document carries one. Each entry holds:

  • its number
  • the question
  • why it matters
  • the options offered
  • the answer or choice
  • the date it was given
  • every place it is used
  • its status:
    • open
    • answered
    • assumed
    • carried
    • closed

Each acceptance is an entry too, with its date and the version accepted.

Every route back

Every route back follows "Sending back". If the target is not yet accepted, its own step settles it. If it is accepted, it goes through change management.
