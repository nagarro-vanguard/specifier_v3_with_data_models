Specifier Workflow Swimlane

The whole workflow in one figure: Phases 1 to 4, the side entrance for artifacts that skipped steps, then change management, all connected. Read it from top to bottom. Each lane shows who does the work. Each document has a rail running down to every step it feeds.

Which skill updates which document

In change management, the landing document's own skill drafts the update, and each linked step re-runs its own skill, with that skill's own stop. Records are never rewritten: a change adds a new entry instead.
