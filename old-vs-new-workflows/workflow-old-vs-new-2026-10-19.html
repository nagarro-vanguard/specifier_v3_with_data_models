Old vs New Workflow

What the toolkit does today, set against the redesigned workflow, row by row. Each row gives the reason for the change and the rationale for the answer, with who decided it. An earlier drawing, the Specifier Workflow Swimlane, predates several of these decisions.

What to do with this page
  • Read each row: old, new, reason, rationale.
  • Rows marked My proposal were never put to you as a question. Challenge any of them in chat by row number.
  • Every open question is now settled. Challenge any row in chat by its number.
"Old" is your existing toolkit, read from its files; each old cell names its source. Nothing in those files has been changed.
